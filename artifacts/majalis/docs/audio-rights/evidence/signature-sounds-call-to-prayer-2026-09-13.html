Call To Prayer Recordings

£0.00

This pack is a collection of authentic Islamic call to prayer (Adhan) recordings captured in Albania, Morocco, and Kosovo, designed to add cultural authenticity, atmosphere, and a sense of place to your creative projects.

Recorded on location in cities, towns, and villages, these sounds capture the natural ambience of the Adhan echoing across streets, neighbourhoods, mountains, and urban landscapes. Each recording preserves the unique acoustics, environmental textures, and local character surrounding the call to prayer, including distant traffic, birds, marketplaces, and everyday life.

From peaceful dawn prayers and quiet evening calls to vibrant city recordings and atmospheric village soundscapes, every recording provides versatile source material for producers, filmmakers, game developers, and sound designers. The sounds work equally well as standalone ambience or layered into larger scenes to create believable and immersive environments.

Whether you're producing films, documentaries, video games, travel content, ambient music, or cinematic sound design, these recordings deliver authentic call to prayer soundscapes with exceptional realism and cultural richness.

  • Natural environmental ambience and location-specific acoustics
