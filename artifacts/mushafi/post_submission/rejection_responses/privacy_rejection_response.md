# Privacy Rejection Response Template

## Situation

Use this if the store reviewer rejects the app due to privacy disclosure, microphone usage, audio upload, or data safety mismatch.

## Response Template - English

Hello,

Thank you for the review.

The app uses the microphone only when the user explicitly starts a recitation review session. Audio upload is disabled by default.

If the user enables the advanced server-based recitation engine and explicitly allows audio upload, the recording may be sent to the configured ASR server for technical recitation review. This behavior is explained in the app privacy screen and the privacy policy.

The app also works without a server for local features, and server-based audio upload can be disabled in settings.

We have reviewed and updated the privacy disclosures to reflect this behavior accurately.

Thank you.
