# Launch Announcement - English

Mushafi for Quran recitation review is now available.

The app helps users review Quran memorization through structured recitation sessions, clear session reports, smart revision planning, daily goals, and reminders.

Notice:
Recitation results are approximate technical aids for review. They are not religious rulings or definitive judgments on recitation or tajweed.

Privacy:
The microphone is used only when a recitation session starts. Audio is not sent to a server unless the user explicitly enables this in settings.
