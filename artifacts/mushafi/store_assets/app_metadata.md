## Target Audience

Users who want to review Quran memorization using structured recitation sessions.

## Important Disclaimer

The app provides approximate technical feedback for memorization review. It does not provide religious rulings, fatwas, or definitive judgments on tajweed or recitation validity.

## Quran Text

The Quran text is bundled in the app and is not generated by artificial intelligence. The publisher must verify that the Quran data file is from a trusted and licensed source before release.

## Audio Upload

Audio is not uploaded by default. Audio may be sent to a configured ASR server only if the user enables the advanced recitation engine and explicitly allows upload.
