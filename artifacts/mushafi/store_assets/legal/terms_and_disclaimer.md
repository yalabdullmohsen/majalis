# Terms and Disclaimer

## Purpose

This app is designed to help users review Quran memorization through structured recitation sessions and technical feedback.

## No Religious Ruling

The app does not provide religious rulings, fatwas, or authoritative judgments on recitation or tajweed.

## Approximate Results

All recitation results, accuracy percentages, highlighted words, and review suggestions are approximate technical aids. They may be affected by microphone quality, background noise, pronunciation, device recognition capabilities, or server availability.

## Quran Data

The Quran text must be verified by the publisher before release. The app must not generate Quran text using artificial intelligence.

## Audio Processing

Audio processing may occur on-device or through a configured server if enabled by the user. Server-based processing should use secure transport and should not retain audio unless clearly disclosed and consented to.

## User Responsibility

Users should consult qualified teachers or trusted sources for religious and tajweed guidance.
